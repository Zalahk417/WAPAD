This page describes the current Cloudflare-hosted pre-launch website. A final production privacy policy will be published before WAPAD connects a server-side customer intake or account system.

01CURRENT TOOL

Assessment answers stay in your browser

The current pre-assessment does not transmit form answers to WAPAD. If you choose “Save browser draft”, the text answers are stored in your device’s browser storage so the draft can be resumed on that browser.

You control the draft

  • You can delete the saved draft from the assessment page.
  • Clearing site data in your browser also removes the local draft.
  • WAPAD cannot retrieve a browser-local draft from another device.
02PHOTOS

Selected images are previewed locally

When you select photographs in the current assessment, the browser creates local previews so you can check what you selected. The image files are not uploaded to WAPAD and are not saved with the browser draft.

Current boundary

  • No WAPAD image database is connected.
  • No cloud case file is created.
  • Refreshing or closing the page removes those local previews.
03TECHNICAL

Hosting, caching and basic requests

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Standard web requests necessarily expose technical request information to the hosting infrastructure. A small service worker may cache WAPAD’s public page files on your device for faster repeat loading; it does not intentionally cache your form answers or selected photos.

What WAPAD has not added

  • No advertising pixels.
  • No WAPAD analytics package.
  • No marketing cookies.
  • No customer login or server-side profile.
04PRODUCTION

Before secure intake goes live

Before WAPAD connects customer accounts, cloud case storage, appointment booking or evidence upload, the final policy should explain what is collected, why it is collected, where it is stored, retention, access and correction rights, disclosure, security controls and the contact process for privacy requests.

Desk ruleDo not quietly turn a local preparation tool into a customer database. The privacy boundary should change only when the operating controls change with it.
