This page describes the current GitHub Pages draft. It is not a substitute for a final Australian Privacy Policy once WAPA begins collecting customer data through a production intake system.

Current assessment form: information entered into the first-pass assessment stays in your browser. The site does not send the form data or selected images to WAPA.

What this draft stores

The current site does not use a WAPA database, account login or server-side customer profile. Assessment fields are processed in the browser only. If you copy, download or create an email draft, you choose where that information goes next.

Photos

Photo selection in the draft only reads the file names needed to build your local case summary. The image files themselves are not uploaded by this site.

Third-party requests

The website uses Google Fonts and openly licensed images hosted by Wikimedia Commons. Loading those resources may cause your browser to make requests to those providers, which can include standard technical information such as IP address and browser details.

Analytics and cookies

No WAPA analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies are intentionally included in this draft.

Production launch

Before a live customer intake system is connected, WAPA should publish a final privacy policy covering the information collected, purpose, storage, retention, access, correction, disclosure, security controls and contact process applicable to the operating business.

High-value asset privacy

For a precious-asset business, privacy is also a physical-security issue. The operating design favours limited disclosure of customer holdings, appointment-based handling, controlled custody records and no unnecessary publication of asset details.
