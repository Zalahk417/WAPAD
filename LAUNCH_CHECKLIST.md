# WAPAD — Controlled Pilot Launch Checklist

This checklist separates **website-ready** work from the legal, operational and commercial controls required before WAPAD accepts assets or makes purchase offers. It is a working project checklist, not legal or tax advice.

- [x] Replace the generic header seal with the supplied WAPAD gold emblem treatment.
- [x] Increase small body, navigation, trust, assessment and footer typography without changing the overall proportions.
- [x] Keep the first-pass assessment local to the browser during pre-launch.

- [ ] Choose and connect the production domain.
- [ ] Create a branded WAPAD intake email address.

- [ ] Add production canonical URL, Open Graph image, sitemap and public robots policy when indexing is enabled.
- [ ] Test desktop, iPhone/iPad, Android, Safari, Chrome, keyboard-only navigation and reduced-motion mode.

## 2. WA second-hand dealer controls

- [ ] Confirm the operating entity and licence holder.
- [ ] Lodge and receive the required WA Second-Hand Dealer Licence before buying, selling or exchanging second-hand goods.
- [ ] Confirm every business/storage premises covered by the licence.
- [ ] Build the required transaction and identity record workflow before the first purchase.
- [ ] Build the 14-day retention workflow: acquired second-hand goods remain unchanged for at least 14 days and remain at licensed business/storage premises during that period.
- [ ] Define police hold/stop-dealing and stolen-property escalation procedures.

Primary sources: WA Police licensing page and the Pawnbrokers and Second-hand Dealers Act 1994 (WA), especially records and sections 61–62.

## 3. Trade measurement and testing

- [ ] Purchase a scale type approved for trade use by the National Measurement Institute.
- [ ] For precious metals under 1 kg, confirm the scale's verification interval (`e`) does not exceed 10 mg.
- [ ] Have the scale verified by an NMI servicing licensee before trade use.
- [ ] Arrange the workstation so the customer can see the weighing process, or provide the required written statement of weight.
- [ ] Establish zero/level/daily test-weight checks and a verification/repair log.
- [ ] Document the layered testing sequence: visual/hallmark inspection → magnet/dimensions/weight → touchstone where appropriate → density/specialist/refinery/XRF escalation where warranted.
- [ ] Document acid handling, PPE, ventilation, neutralisation, storage and spill procedures.
- [ ] Define items that WAPAD will not authenticate or value without specialist referral.

Primary sources: National Measurement Institute precious-metals trade measurement guidance; GIA touchstone guidance.

## 4. Payment and AML/CTF boundary

- [ ] Adopt **bank-transfer-only settlement at launch**; do not accept or pay physical currency or virtual assets unless the AML/CTF consequences have been reviewed and the required controls are in place.
- [ ] Document how staff identify linked or apparently linked transactions.
- [ ] Confirm with professional advice whether any planned WAPAD service is an AUSTRAC designated service.
- [ ] If WAPAD will provide a designated service, complete AUSTRAC enrolment and implement the applicable AML/CTF program, customer due diligence, reporting, staff training and recordkeeping before providing it.

Current AUSTRAC guidance states that the newly regulated precious-metals/stones designated service is triggered by purchases or sales of regulated items valued at $10,000 or more where the relevant payment involves physical currency and/or virtual assets, including linked transactions. Purchases or sales using only debit/credit card or bank transfer are not that designated service. This boundary must be rechecked if the payment model changes.

## 5. Security, custody and insurance

- [ ] Obtain insurer approval for the operating/storage model before holding customer or purchased assets.
- [ ] Install the required rated safe, CCTV, alarm and duress controls.
- [ ] Use appointment-only access for the pilot.
- [ ] Do not advertise or hold transaction cash at the desk.
- [ ] Use tamper-evident bags and unique case/transaction IDs.
- [ ] Document custody transfer, safe access and two-person controls for higher-value parcels.
- [ ] Set maximum value per appointment and maximum aggregate overnight holding limits.
- [ ] Create robbery, theft, fire, lost-property and emergency-response procedures.

## 6. Commercial controls before the first purchase

- [ ] Open and test at least two executable exit channels/refinery or specialist buyer pathways.
- [ ] Confirm actual settlement, assay, freight and refining deductions in writing.
- [ ] Lock the customer-offer calculation to **net recoverable value**, not public spot alone.
- [ ] Define maximum payout percentages, volatility reserve and bad-buy reserve.
- [ ] Set per-customer and daily working-capital exposure limits.
- [ ] Define quote expiry times for volatile precious-metal prices.
- [ ] Obtain written accounting/GST treatment advice for second-hand goods and valuable metals.
- [ ] Finalise receipt, purchase record, customer explanation and written-calculation templates.

## 7. Pilot gate

WAPAD should launch as a **controlled appointment-based pilot**, not as a capital-heavy retail store.

- [ ] Licence, scale verification, insurance/security and operating procedures complete.
- [ ] Secure intake email and booking workflow tested end-to-end.
- [ ] Conduct at least five dry-run assessments using known/reference items before taking a customer purchase.
- [ ] Conduct one complete mock transaction: booking → ID/records → visible weighing/testing → written calculation → payment → custody → 14-day retention → exit channel → reconciliation.
- [ ] Obtain a final professional compliance/tax review of the operating workflow.
- [ ] Remove the website's pre-launch/noindex state only when the above launch-critical controls are complete.
- [ ] Run a 90-day pilot with explicit transaction, gross-profit, testing-loss, referral and customer-acquisition metrics before increasing fixed costs or buying premium equipment.
