Decision Brief

For one watch, jewellery piece, bullion item or other supported precious asset where the free Route Check has passed Evidence Ready and exposed a question worth researching — but you do not automatically need a formal valuation.

What you receive

  • Machine-prepared case from the Evidence Ready Route Check.
  • Human review of the supplied evidence, with a 12–15 minute target review window.
  • Clear statement of what is known, inferred and still unknown.
  • Relevant market-route research and comparable references where useful and available.
  • Intrinsic-value context when it materially affects the route.
  • Comparison of plausible routes: hold, insure, intact resale, auction/consignment, metal route or specialist examination.
  • Confidence notes, evidence gaps and a concise written next-step recommendation.
  • One targeted retake request if one material image is still missing.

What it is not

  • Not a registered jewellery valuation or insurance valuation.
  • Not authentication, gemstone grading or a watch condition report.
  • Not a purchase offer from WAPAD.
  • Not a guarantee that a comparable sale can be repeated.
  • Not an open-ended research engagement.
  • Not a substitute for physical inspection where the evidence requires one.

Estate Triage

For inherited jewellery boxes, watch groups, mixed precious-metal collections or family assets where the first problem is deciding what deserves deeper attention before items are cleaned, split, sold or formally valued.

What you receive

  • Structured triage of up to 10 items for the pilot scope.
  • Priority map: investigate now, preserve together, simple market/metal route, or low-priority.
  • Flags for signed pieces, watches, coins, stones, documents and matched sets that may justify specialist work.
  • Evidence-gap checklist for the highest-priority pieces.
  • Recommendation on which items justify a Decision Brief, registered valuer, watch specialist, gemmologist or auction route.
  • One consolidated written triage report for the collection.

What it is not

  • Not 10 formal valuations bundled into one fee.
  • Not probate, tax, legal or equitable-distribution advice.
  • Not remote authentication of every item.
  • Not custody or collection by WAPAD during the digital pilot.
  • Third-party specialist or laboratory fees are separate if required.
