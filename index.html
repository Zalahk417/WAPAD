02 · The WAPAD method

Proof before price.
Route before pressure.

The same four-stage decision chain applies to every asset. Complexity is added only when the value at risk justifies it.

01

Identify

Record what is visible before testing changes the object: marks, maker, reference, construction, condition, papers and provenance.

WHAT IS IT?
02

Verify

Use the least destructive reliable method first, then escalate to trade measurement, specialist analysis, XRF or referral when warranted.

WHAT IS PROVABLE?
03

Value

Separate intrinsic value, executable market evidence, collectable premium and replacement or utility context.

WHAT SUPPORTS VALUE?
04

Route

Hold, insure, sell intact, refine, consign, auction or refer. The fastest transaction is not automatically the best outcome.

WHAT NEXT?
00
Before all four steps

Preserve the asset.

Do not polish, separate, melt, resize, open, acid-test or discard packaging/papers simply to “find out what it is”. Evidence destroyed before identification cannot be recovered later.

03 · Value anatomy

A number without a method
is just a number.

A trustworthy assessment explains where the value is coming from and what could change it. WAPAD’s model keeps four value layers separate so one does not silently erase another.

04 · Route map

The outcome is not always
“sell it”.

WAPAD is designed to make the route explicit. That means being able to say “hold”, “refer” or “sell it another way” when that is the evidence-led answer.

ASSET
01

Hold / insure

When family significance, replacement cost or future optionality outweighs liquidity.

02

Sell intact

When the whole item has a defensible market premium over material value.

03

Refine / metal route

When recoverable material is the dominant value and collectable premium is weak.

04

Consign / auction

When specialist reach and patient price discovery matter more than speed.

05

Specialist referral

When uncertainty or value-at-risk exceeds the desk’s competence or equipment.
