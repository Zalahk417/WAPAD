05 · Trust architecture

Trust is a
process.

WAPA should never ask a customer to trust a black box. The trust design is built from visible measurement, explainable testing, written records and clear licensing boundaries.

Pre-launch status: direct dealing and trade-measurement services will only be offered once required licences, verified equipment and procedures are in place.

For precious metals under 1 kg, NMI rules set a maximum 10 mg verification scale interval. Approved and verified scales must be used correctly; customers should be able to see the weighing process, or receive a written statement of weight.

National Measurement Institute ↗

Touchstone comparison is a practical, relatively non-destructive method for karat gold, but high-value or ambiguous pieces should not depend on a single screening method. The operating design uses layered checks and specialist/refinery analysis where needed.

GIA touchstone method ↗

In Western Australia, a Second-Hand Dealer Licence covers buying, selling or exchanging second-hand goods. Pawnbroking is different. WAPA’s planned launch model is acquisition-focused and does not present itself as a pawnbroker.

WA Police licensing ↗

The operating concept is designed around publishing the calculation method, preserving evidence and providing a written breakdown so a customer can understand the difference between gross metal value, recoverable value and the actual offer.

The pilot design favours appointment-only access, no advertised cash holdings, bank transfer, tamper-evident custody, secure storage and escalation controls for larger parcels.
