01 — The desk

A precious asset is rarely
just its weight.

The fastest quote is not always the best answer. WAPA starts by separating the layers that can create — or destroy — value.

Metal purity and weight matter. So do maker, reference, condition, rarity, workmanship, provenance, demand and the route you choose to sell through.

04 — Standards & trust

Trust is a
process.

Precious-asset work sits at the intersection of measurement, testing, valuation and second-hand dealing. WAPA’s operating model is being built around the rules that apply to each activity — not around shortcuts.

WAPA is in development. Direct dealing and trade-measurement services will only be offered where the required licences, verified equipment and procedures are in place.
01

Trade measurement

Where precious metals are bought or valued by weight, approved and correctly verified measuring instruments matter.

Second-hand dealing
