07 · Common questions

Before you
send anything anywhere.

These are the questions the quick-answer button is built to handle too.

Do you buy gold or jewellery?＋

Not in the controlled digital pilot. WAPAD currently helps organise evidence and choose the next route. If a future buying service is launched, it will be clearly separated from decision support and subject to the required licensing and controls.

Can you tell me what my watch is worth?＋

The free Route Check can help identify what evidence and market matter next. A defensible market estimate or formal valuation may require reference confirmation, originality checks, physical examination or a qualified specialist.

I’m not in Western Australia. Can I still use WAPAD?＋

Yes for the digital Route Check. It is browser-based and location-light. Physical inspections, named referrals and regulated services depend on your location and the type of asset.

Do my photos get uploaded?＋

Not in the current free Route Check. Selected images are previewed locally in your browser and are not transmitted to WAPAD.

Why not just go straight to a jeweller, buyer or auction house?＋

Sometimes that is exactly the right next step. WAPAD is useful when you are unsure which one is appropriate, whether the item warrants specialist cost, or whether a different market could materially change the outcome.
