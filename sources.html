Sources / verification

Sources,
not slogans.

Key compliance and testing statements used in WAPAD’s launch design are linked back to the underlying regulator or specialist source.

01MEASUREMENT

National Measurement Institute

Trade measurement requirements relevant to buying, selling and valuing precious metals and stones by measurement, including approved/verified scales, verification intervals and customer visibility of weighing.

Primary sourceOpen NMI guidance ↗
02WA LICENSING

WA Police — Pawnbroker and Second-Hand Dealer licensing

Western Australian licensing categories and the distinction between second-hand dealing and pawnbroking.

Primary sourceOpen WA Police guidance ↗
03TESTING

Gemological Institute of America

Bench guidance on the touchstone method for determining karat-gold purity and its practical testing context.

Specialist sourceOpen GIA guidance ↗
04AML / CTF

AUSTRAC — Precious metals, stones and products

Current guidance for newly regulated dealers, including the relevant designated-service boundary, linked transactions and payment-method context.

Primary sourceOpen AUSTRAC guidance ↗
05WA LAW

Pawnbrokers and Second-hand Dealers Act 1994 (WA)

Primary legislation covering records and retention, including the requirements around keeping acquired second-hand goods unchanged for the statutory retention period and at licensed premises.

Primary sourceOpen WA legislation ↗
06ASSET MANIFEST

Legacy image source manifest

Earlier WAPAD design drafts used selected Wikimedia Commons imagery. The v2 core interface is deliberately self-contained and does not depend on those third-party hero/gallery images, but the existing manifest remains available for provenance and licence records.
